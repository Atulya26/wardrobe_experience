# Asset notes

Room photographs, garment visuals and wearer images are supplied illustrative prototype assets, including AI-generated imagery. They are not body scans or measurements. The different wearer views in Flow 1 are image frames, not a continuous 3D body model.

The symbols under each app's public/assets/symbols/ directory are Apple SF Symbols rendered through macOS AppKit NSImage(systemSymbolName:) at medium weight as transparent PNG masks. Source: https://developer.apple.com/sf-symbols/ . They are existing Apple-platform prototype assets, not original project artwork or a newly licensed cross-platform icon set. System typography uses the local system font stack rather than shipping Apple font files.

No source research PDFs, original exported ZIPs, private screenshots or credentials are included in this deployment repository.
